## Edition and term evidence

- The working English witness is F. Eden Pargiter's *The Markandeya Purana*, published by the Asiatic Society of Bengal in Calcutta in 1904. Bibliographic record and scan: <https://archive.org/details/cu31924022991974>.
- A contemporary Royal Asiatic Society obituary records Pargiter's death on 18 February 1927: <https://doi.org/10.1017/S0035869X00058019>.
- Section 22 of India's Copyright Act provides the assessed life-plus-sixty-year term for published literary works: <https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1367/1/a195714.pdf>. On that basis, the assessed Indian term ended on 31 December 1987.
- U.S. Copyright Office Circular 38B explains both the source-country condition for URAA restoration and the general 95-year term for restored pre-1978 works: <https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ38b.pdf>. Either assessed route places the 1904 Pargiter edition outside copyright term in the United States.

The determination does not authorize copying the Wisdomlib presentation, its synopsis or OCR, or the SanskritDocuments transcription. The project uses the locked graph's independently written paraphrases, stable evidence IDs, and locators. SanskritDocuments remains reference-only under its published copying guidance: <https://www.sanskritdocuments.org/faq/>.
