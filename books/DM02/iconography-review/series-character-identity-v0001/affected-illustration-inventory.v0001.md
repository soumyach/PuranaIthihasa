## Disposition definitions

- `PRESERVE_AUDIT_ONLY`: immutable prior/superseded version; never delete, overwrite, or spend a correction operation on it.
- `LOCALIZED_CHARACTER_REPLACEMENT_CANDIDATE`: source event and most non-character pixels remain usable, but the Brahma/Vishnu region must be replaced or materially corrected after reference approval. This is a production-planning judgment, not a claim that a future image edit will succeed.
- `FULL_REGENERATION_REQUIRED`: the divine identity change affects the composition too broadly, or the image already has a source/action defect that cannot be repaired honestly through layout, crop, or lettering.
- `FULL_REGENERATION_AFTER_QUALIFIED_REVIEW`: regeneration is necessary, but the existing religious/iconography gate must be cleared first.

No existing complete Brahma/Vishnu raster is approved for unchanged final reuse. Prior `PASS` results remain valid within their recorded prompt scope; the user's later rejection creates a new series-identity blocker and does not falsify or erase those reviews.
