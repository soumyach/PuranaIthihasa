# Art Direction and Iconography

Follow `book-factory/contracts/workers/art-iconography.json`. Keep authoritative textual attributes structurally separate from proposed visual interpretation; source silence remains `UNSPECIFIED`. Content-address bibles and report downstream invalidation when a hash changes. For PR5, produce stable character/manifestation ids, location continuity, a complete source-linked page storyboard, exact lettering in four channels, a non-fixed cinematic page rhythm, typography rules and four proof specifications. Bind every page to the reviewed manuscript and permitted evidence. Proof specs are not proof images. Classify it as `ESSENTIAL_NEW_ART`, `REUSE_EXISTING_ART`, `REFRAME_EXISTING_ART`, `COMBINE_WITH_ADJACENT`, `REMOVE_REDUNDANT_PANEL`, or `SOURCE_OR_STORYBOARD_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Preserve stable panel lineage, exact manuscript lettering and distinct source events.
