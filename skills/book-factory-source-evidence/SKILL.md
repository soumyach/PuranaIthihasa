# Source and Evidence Review

Follow `book-factory/contracts/workers/source-evidence.json`. Query only through the read-only adapter, preserve every graph ID, and run the existing validator before and after reading. Assess bounded-arc completeness, chapter extraction, source examination, critical flags, and rights independently. A `COMPLETE` spine proves only its bounded chronology. In the Story Packet, cite every critical source fact and leave missing motivation, dialogue, iconography, chronology, or causality `UNSPECIFIED`. Return `BLOCKED`, `INCOMPLETE_SOURCE`, or `NEEDS_SOURCE_REVIEW` instead of guessing, and never approve your own work.

Bind shared spines to the candidate's exact event subset and include witness/rights evidence. Keep controlled-pilot eligibility separate from source certification and publication readiness.

A private-draft clearance is a separate immutable record, not a dossier override. It must distinguish the ancient narrative, the selected edition/translation, and modern digital presentation rights; cite the actual rights/provenance evidence; record an independent pivotal-claim review; prohibit reuse outside the assessed boundary; and keep publication blocked wherever jurisdiction, collation, or theological approval remains unresolved.

For a PR5 `SOURCE_FIDELITY` manuscript review, independently read the complete immutable manuscript against the locked packet and claim map. Provide written PASS, FIX, or ESCALATE assessments for complete event coverage, factual/theological accuracy, identity accuracy, causality, framing, quotation/source notes, and ending. Check every required event individually. This review does not change source readiness, rights, theological authority, or publication readiness.

In PR9 orchestration, return an exact job receipt with the graph, mapping, dossier, packet, policy and clearance hashes. Reuse certified source material only at the same evidence, source-scope, tradition and edition boundary. A changed source hash invalidates its manuscript and later dependents; it does not erase unrelated catalogue or reference records. Never let a bounded-arc PASS imply private-draft permission or public-publication clearance.
