### 1. Suratha remains attached after dispossession.

Mārkaṇḍeya introduces the future eighth Manu. Suratha of Caitra’s line loses to hostile kings, returns to his own city and resumes ruling his own territory, then suffers renewed enemy attack. His powerful corrupt ministers seize treasury and army even in his city. Riding away under a hunting pretext, he reaches Medhas’s hermitage yet worries about kingdom, elephant, servants and treasure.

**Outcome:** Suratha remains attached after dispossession.

**Participants:** Mārkaṇḍeya, Suratha, Caitra lineage, Kolāvidhvaṃsin hostile kings, Suratha’s corrupt ministers, Suratha’s chief elephant, Medhas, Sāvarṇi / Sāvarṇika Manu, Sūrya / Vivasvat, Svārociṣa period

### 2. Their shared attachment becomes the question.

Suratha asks the distressed merchant his identity. Samādhi says wife, sons and kinsmen expelled him for wealth; he still worries about their welfare. Suratha questions this affection; Samādhi admits knowledge has not made him unloving.

**Outcome:** Their shared attachment becomes the question.

**Participants:** Suratha, Samādhi, Samādhi’s wife, sons and kinsmen

- [DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 62–69](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117155.html) · evidence `dm-evidence-002` · DIRECT_TEXT/VERIFIED

### 3. Medhas begins Devī’s narratives.

They approach Medhas respectfully. Suratha asks why discrimination does not end attachment. Medhas cites hungry birds feeding young and explains Mahāmāyā’s power over even knowers; propitiated, she also grants liberating knowledge. Suratha asks her origin, nature and activity.

**Outcome:** Medhas begins Devī’s narratives.

**Participants:** Suratha, Samādhi, Medhas, Yoganidrā / Mahāmāyā

- [DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 72–85](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117155.html) · evidence `dm-evidence-003` · DIRECT_TEXT/VERIFIED

### 62. Their worship pleases Devī.

Medhas urges refuge in Devī, giver of enjoyment, heaven and release. Suratha bows and joins Samādhi on a riverbank; they make an earthen image, recite, offer flowers, incense, fire and water, restrict food and give blood-sprinkled offerings for three years.

**Outcome:** Their worship pleases Devī.

**Participants:** Medhas, Suratha, Samādhi, Devī / Caṇḍikā, Unnamed river sand-bank

- [DM 13 / Pargiter canto 93, web lines 53–57](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117175.html) · evidence `dm-evidence-062` · DIRECT_TEXT/VERIFIED

### 63. The two devotees request different goals.

Devī appears and offers their desired gifts. Suratha requests regained rule with enemies defeated now and lasting kingship in another birth; disillusioned Samādhi requests knowledge removing attachment to I and mine.

**Outcome:** The two devotees request different goals.

**Participants:** Devī / Caṇḍikā, Suratha, Samādhi

- [DM 13 / Pargiter canto 93, web lines 58–63](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117175.html) · evidence `dm-evidence-063` · DIRECT_TEXT/VERIFIED

### 64. Both boons are granted; the frame closes.

Devī promises Suratha his kingdom within days after killing enemies, then birth from Vivasvat as Manu Sāvarṇi; she grants Samādhi perfected knowledge. Praised by both, she disappears. Mārkaṇḍeya reiterates Suratha’s future birth as the eighth Manu.

**Outcome:** Both boons are granted; the frame closes.

**Participants:** Devī / Caṇḍikā, Suratha, Samādhi, Sūrya / Vivasvat, Sāvarṇi / Sāvarṇika Manu
