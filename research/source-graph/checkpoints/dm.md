## Source-ordered narrative

### DM 1 / Mārkaṇḍeya 81

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117155.html)

- **dm-event-001** — Mārkaṇḍeya introduces the future eighth Manu. Suratha of Caitra’s line loses to hostile kings, returns to his own city and resumes ruling his own territory, then suffers renewed enemy attack. His powerful corrupt ministers seize treasury and army even in his city. Riding away under a hunting pretext, he reaches Medhas’s hermitage yet worries about kingdom, elephant, servants and treasure. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto81, web lines57–61)
- **dm-event-002** — Suratha asks the distressed merchant his identity. Samādhi says wife, sons and kinsmen expelled him for wealth; he still worries about their welfare. Suratha questions this affection; Samādhi admits knowledge has not made him unloving. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 62–69)
- **dm-event-003** — They approach Medhas respectfully. Suratha asks why discrimination does not end attachment. Medhas cites hungry birds feeding young and explains Mahāmāyā’s power over even knowers; propitiated, she also grants liberating knowledge. Suratha asks her origin, nature and activity. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 72–85)
- **dm-event-004** — Medhas distinguishes eternal Devī from births described when she appears for divine purposes. At cosmic dissolution Viṣṇu sleeps on Śeṣa; Madhu and Kaiṭabha arise from his ear-region and threaten lotus-seated Brahmā. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 87–89)
- **dm-event-005** — Brahmā praises the goddess as sacrificial utterance, cosmic creation, preservation and dissolution, knowledge, illusion, nature and armed power. He asks her to delude the demons and awaken Viṣṇu to kill them. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 90–96)
- **dm-event-006** — Yoganidrā leaves Viṣṇu’s eyes, mouth, nose, arms, heart and breast and appears before Brahmā. Viṣṇu awakens and fights the demons with his arms for five thousand years; deluded and intoxicated with strength, they offer him a boon. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 98–99)
- **dm-event-007** — Viṣṇu chooses their death at his hands. They stipulate a place where earth is not flooded; he accepts and kills them with his discus. The Sanskrit says jaghane, while Pargiter renders the bodily locus ambiguously; a precise thigh-position is reserved for review. (DM 1 / Pargiter canto 81, web lines 100–109)

### DM 2 / Mārkaṇḍeya 82

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117157.html)

- **dm-event-008** — After a hundred-year war Mahiṣa wins Indra’s office and divine jurisdictions; displaced gods roam earth. Led by Brahmā, they tell Śiva and Viṣṇu their defeat and seek a plan to kill him. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 55–57)
- **dm-event-009** — Divine energies converge as Devī: Śiva’s face, Yama’s hair, Viṣṇu’s arms, Moon’s breasts, Indra’s waist, Varuṇa’s legs/thighs, Earth’s hips, Brahmā’s feet, Sun’s toes, Vasus’ fingers, Kubera’s nose, Prajāpati’s teeth, Agni’s eyes, twilights’ brows and wind’s ears. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 58–60)
- **dm-event-010** — Śiva gives a trident; Viṣṇu a discus; Varuṇa a conch and noose; Agni a spear; wind a bow and full quivers; Indra a vajra and Airāvata’s bell; Yama a rod; Prajāpati a rosary; Brahmā a water-pot; Sun rays; Kāla sword and shield. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 61–62)
- **dm-event-011** — Milk Ocean supplies pearl necklace and imperishable garments; ornaments adorn Devī. Viśvakarman gives axe, diverse weapons and impenetrable armor; Ocean lotus garlands and lotus; Himavat lion and gems; Kubera a wine cup; Śeṣa a jeweled serpent necklace. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 62–64)
- **dm-event-012** — Devī’s roar shakes worlds, sea, earth and mountains; gods and sages praise her. Mahiṣa musters armies and approaches its source. Her light, crown, bow sound and thousand arms fill the worlds; Cikṣura and Cāmara enter battle. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 64–68)
- **dm-event-013** — Udagra, Mahāhanu, Asiloman, Bāṣkala, Biḍāla and huge contingents fight with missile and close weapons; Pargiter inserts Ugradarśana from Bombay’s reading where the displayed Sanskrit has no proper name. Mahiṣa leads enormous chariot, elephant and horse forces. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 69–71)
- **dm-event-014** — Devī cuts enemy missiles effortlessly. Her lion attacks; her breaths create energized troops carrying axes, javelins, swords and halberds, with drums and conchs. Her weapons kill and bind enemies; bell sound confounds them. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 72–75)
- **dm-event-015** — Mutilated demons and armed headless bodies continue fighting or dance to martial music. Fallen forces obstruct the field and blood forms rivers. Devī, lion and emanated troops destroy the host; gods shower flowers. (DM 2 / Pargiter canto 82, web lines 76–79)

### DM 3 / Mārkaṇḍeya 83

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117158.html)

- **dm-event-016** — Cikṣura shoots arrows; Devī cuts them, kills horses and driver, breaks bow and banner and wounds him. His sword strikes lion and Devī’s arm, then breaks. His thrown śūla and his body are shattered by hers. (DM 3 / Pargiter canto 83, web lines 55–58)
- **dm-event-017** — Elephant-mounted Cāmara hurls a spear; Devī’s huṃ renders it powerless, and arrows cut his next śūla. Lion leaps onto elephant, wrestles Cāmara on the ground, then springs and severs his head with a paw-blow. (DM 3 / Pargiter canto 83, web lines 59–60)
- **dm-event-018** — Devī kills Udagra with stones/trees, Karāla with bodily blows, Uddhata with club, Bāṣkala with bhindipāla, Tāmra/Andhaka with arrows, Ugrāsya/Ugravīrya/Mahāhanu with trident, Biḍāla with sword and Durdhara/Durmukha with arrows. (DM 3 / Pargiter canto 83, web lines 61–62)
- **dm-event-019** — Buffalo-shaped Mahiṣa attacks troops with muzzle, hooves, tail, horns, rush, roar, circling and breath, then attacks lion. His movements shake earth, sea, clouds and mountains. (DM 3 / Pargiter canto 83, web lines 63–65)
- **dm-event-020** — Devī binds Mahiṣa. He changes buffalo to lion, then armed man as she cuts at him; arrows strike man, sword and shield. Elephant-form seizes lion; she cuts its trunk. He becomes buffalo again. (DM 3 / Pargiter canto 83, web lines 66–67)
- **dm-event-021** — Devī drinks and laughs; arrows break hurled mountains. She tells Mahiṣa his roaring is brief, leaps, presses his neck and strikes with śūla. As he emerges halfway from the buffalo mouth, she beheads him. Gods rejoice; celestial singers and dancers celebrate. (DM 3 / Pargiter canto 83, web lines 67–73)

### DM 4 / Mārkaṇḍeya 84

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117161.html)

- **dm-event-022** — Gods praise Devī’s cosmic power, knowledge, prosperity, protection and mercy in battle, including the claim that slain enemies reach heaven. They ask her gentle and fearsome forms and her weapons to protect every direction. (DM 4 / Pargiter canto 84, web lines 56–152)
- **dm-event-023** — Gods offer flowers, perfumes and incense. Invited to choose a boon, they ask relief whenever they remember her in calamity and prosperity for mortals singing this hymn. She agrees and disappears; Medhas introduces her later emergence from Gaurī. (DM 4 / Pargiter canto 84, web lines 155–167)

### DM 5 / Mārkaṇḍeya 85

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117163.html)

- **dm-event-024** — Śumbha and Niśumbha seize the three worlds, divine jurisdictions and sacrificial portions. Dispossessed gods recall Devī’s earlier promise, go to Himavat and invoke her as Viṣṇu’s māyā. (DM 5 / Pargiter canto 85, web lines 54–55)
- **dm-event-025** — Their hymn salutes Devī in auspicious and fierce modes and as consciousness, intellect, sleep, hunger, shadow, power, thirst, patience, class, modesty, peace, faith, beauty, fortune, activity, memory, mercy, contentment, mother, error, sense-governance and pervading awareness. (DM 5 / Pargiter canto 85, web lines 57–176)
- **dm-event-026** — Pārvatī arrives to bathe and asks whom gods praise. Ambikā emerges from her bodily sheath and answers that the hymn addresses her; she is named Kauśikī. Pārvatī turns dark and is called Kālikā. Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa see Ambikā. (DM 5 / Pargiter canto 85, web lines 178–181)
- **dm-event-027** — Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa commend her beauty and urge seizure alongside Śumbha’s treasures: Airāvata, Pārijāta, Uccaiḥśravas, Brahmā’s vehicle, Mahāpadma, Ocean’s garland, Varuṇa’s umbrella, Prajāpati’s chariot, Utkrāntidā, Varuṇa’s noose held by Niśumbha, sea-gems and Agni’s garments. (DM 5 / Pargiter canto 85, web lines 182–185)
- **dm-event-028** — Śumbha sends Sugrīva to secure willing arrival. The envoy claims Śumbha’s rule, sacrifices and treasures, offers marriage to him or younger Niśumbha and promises incomparable sovereignty. (DM 5 / Pargiter canto 85, web lines 188–192)
- **dm-event-029** — Devī replies that her former vow requires a husband who defeats her in battle, removes her pride and equals her strength. Sugrīva mocks a lone woman’s chance and threatens dragging her by her hair; she repeats the vow and asks him to report it. (DM 5 / Pargiter canto 85, web lines 195–203)

### DM 6 / Mārkaṇḍeya 86

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117165.html)

- **dm-event-030** — Sugrīva reports her response. Angry Śumbha orders Dhūmralocana to drag her by the hair and kill any rescuer, god, Yakṣa or Gandharva. He arrives with sixty thousand Asuras and threatens forcible capture. (DM 6 / Pargiter canto 86, web lines 56–60)
- **dm-event-031** — Devī responds to his boast; when he charges, her huṃ reduces him to ashes. His army showers missiles; her lion kills with paws, jaws and claws, tears bodies and drinks blood, destroying the host. (DM 6 / Pargiter canto 86, web lines 61–67)
- **dm-event-032** — Śumbha orders Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa to bring her bound or dragged by hair; if capture is doubtful, all troops should kill her and the lion, then seize her. (DM 6 / Pargiter canto 86, web lines 68–69)

### DM 7 / Mārkaṇḍeya 87

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117166.html)

- **dm-event-033** — Fourfold forces approach smiling lion-seated Devī on a golden peak. Her angry face darkens; Kālī emerges from her frowning forehead with sword, noose, khaṭvāṅga, skull garland, tiger skin, gaunt body, wide mouth, tongue and sunken red eyes. (DM 7 / Pargiter canto 87, web lines 56–58)
- **dm-event-034** — Kālī devours elephants with crews and bells, horses with fighters, chariots with drivers and enemy weapons; she crushes foes bodily and kills with sword, staff and teeth. (DM 7 / Pargiter canto 87, web lines 59–61)
- **dm-event-035** — Caṇḍa charges, and Muṇḍa showers arrows and discuses. Kālī laughs, seizes Caṇḍa’s hair and cuts his head, then kills Muṇḍa. Survivors flee. Presenting their heads to Caṇḍikā, she reserves Śumbha/Niśumbha for her; Caṇḍikā names her Cāmuṇḍā. (DM 7 / Pargiter canto 87, web lines 62–68)

### DM 8 / Mārkaṇḍeya 88

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117167.html)

- **dm-event-036** — Śumbha musters eighty-six Daityas, eighty-four Kambūs, fifty valiant families, one hundred Dhaumra families, Kālakas, Daurhṛdas, Mauryas and Kālakeyas, and marches. Devī’s bow, bell, lion and Kālī’s cries resound; armies surround them. (DM 8 / Pargiter canto 88, web lines 55–58)
- **dm-event-037** — Divine Śaktis arrive: Brahmāṇī with swans, rosary and pot; Māheśvarī on bull with trident, snakes and moon; Kaumārī on peacock with spear; Vaiṣṇavī on Garuḍa with conch, discus, club, Śārṅga and sword; boar Vārāhī, lion-form Nārasiṃhī and thousand-eyed elephant-riding Aindrī with vajra. (DM 8 / Pargiter canto 88, web lines 59–62)
- **dm-event-038** — Caṇḍikā’s fierce Śakti emerges with a hundred-jackal cry and appoints Śiva envoy: restore Indra’s worlds and divine sacrifice portions, retreat to Pātāla to live, or fight and feed her jackals. Hence Śivadūtī’s name. The Asuras reject the ultimatum and attack. (DM 8 / Pargiter canto 88, web lines 63–65)
- **dm-event-039** — Devī cuts missiles. Kālī strikes with śūla and staff; Brahmāṇī’s water weakens courage and force. Māheśvarī uses trident, Vaiṣṇavī discus, Kaumārī spear, Aindrī vajra; Vārāhī snout, tusks and discus; Nārasiṃhī claws/devouring; Śivadūtī laughter/devouring. Asuras flee the Mothers. (DM 8 / Pargiter canto 88, web lines 65–68)
- **dm-event-040** — Raktabīja advances with club. Each blood-drop falling to earth produces an equal in size and strength. Aindrī’s vajra and club, Vaiṣṇavī’s discus and other Mothers’ weapons cause multiplying blood-born fighters; he clubs them individually. Gods fear the proliferating army. (DM 8 / Pargiter canto 88, web lines 69–72)
- **dm-event-041** — Caṇḍikā directs Cāmuṇḍā to catch the blood and devour generated Asuras. As Devī wounds Raktabīja, Kālī drinks every flow, including fighters generated in her mouth. His club causes Devī no pain; multi-weapon blows drain him bloodless. Gods rejoice and Mothers dance. (DM 8 / Pargiter canto 88, web lines 73–76)

### DM 9 / Mārkaṇḍeya 89

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117169.html)

- **dm-event-042** — Suratha asks what followed Raktabīja’s death. Medhas narrates the brothers’ fury: Niśumbha and Śumbha advance with their armies and shower arrows; Caṇḍikā cuts their arrows and wounds them. (DM 9 / Pargiter canto 89, web lines 56–59)
- **dm-event-043** — Niśumbha strikes lion with sword. Devī’s kṣurapra cuts sword and eight-moon shield; discus cuts spear, fist shatters śūla, trident burns his club to ash, and arrows fell his axe-bearing advance. (DM 9 / Pargiter canto 89, web lines 60–61)
- **dm-event-044** — Eight-armed Śumbha advances. Conch, bow, bell, lion, Kālī’s hand-strikes and Śivadūtī’s laugh terrify forces; Devī challenges him. A great firebrand counters his flaming spear. After reciprocal arrow-cutting, Caṇḍikā’s śūla fells him unconscious. (DM 9 / Pargiter canto 89, web lines 62–66)
- **dm-event-045** — Niśumbha revives and shoots Devī, Kālī and lion. Ten thousand arms throw discuses; Devī cuts these and arrows. She cuts his club with sword and pierces his heart with śūla; another strong man emerges challenging her and is beheaded. (DM 9 / Pargiter canto 89, web lines 67–69)
- **dm-event-046** — Lion, Kālī and Śivadūtī devour troops; Kaumārī’s spear, Brahmāṇī’s mantra-purified water, Māheśvarī’s trident, Vārāhī’s snout, Vaiṣṇavī’s discus and Aindrī’s vajra destroy or repel them. Some die, some flee, some are eaten. (DM 9 / Pargiter canto 89, web lines 70–72)

### DM 10 / Mārkaṇḍeya 90

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117170.html)

- **dm-event-047** — Śumbha grieving his brother accuses Durgā of relying on other goddesses. She states that they are her manifestations, absorbs them into herself and stands alone; the apparent plurality is withdrawn before the duel. (DM 10 / Pargiter canto 90, web lines 56–64)
- **dm-event-048** — Each counters divine missiles; Devī breaks his with huṃ. Arrows break Śumbha’s bow, discus his spear, arrows his sword, hundred-moon shield and mace. Without horse or driver he punches her breast; her palm-blow fells him. (DM 10 / Pargiter canto 90, web lines 66–69)
- **dm-event-049** — Śumbha rises, seizes Devī and ascends. Unsupported she wrestles him in the sky, whirls and throws him down. He charges again with fist raised; her śūla pierces his chest and kills him, shaking earth and seas. (DM 10 / Pargiter canto 90, web lines 69–71)
- **dm-event-050** — The universe clears: fiery portents cease, rivers return to channels, winds and sunlight become auspicious, sacrificial fires burn peacefully, and gods, Gandharvas and Apsarases celebrate. (DM 10 / Pargiter canto 90, web lines 72–73)

### DM 11 / Mārkaṇḍeya 91

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117171.html)

- **dm-event-051** — Agni-led gods sing the Nārāyaṇī hymn: Devī sustains earth and water, deludes and liberates, appears as divine Śaktis, shields suppliants, and guards against enemies and disasters. They ask unceasing protection and pacification for the worlds. (DM 11 / Pargiter canto 91, web lines 56–184)
- **dm-event-052** — Invited to choose a boon, gods request removal of troubles and foes. Devī predicts other Śumbha and Niśumbha in the twenty-eighth age of Vaivasvata’s manvantara; born of Yaśodā in Nanda’s house and residing at Vindhya, she will kill them. (DM 11 / Pargiter canto 91, web lines 185–193)
- **dm-event-053** — Devī predicts a fierce form devouring Vaipracitta Dānavas; their consumption reddens her teeth like pomegranate flowers, producing the name Raktadantikā. (DM 11 / Pargiter canto 91, web lines 194–194)
- **dm-event-054** — After a hundred-year drought, remembered by sages, she will appear without womb-birth as hundred-eyed Śatākṣī, nourish the world with vegetables from her body until rain, become Śākambharī, kill Durgama and be named Durgādevī. Sanskrit clarifies wording omitted or mistranscribed in English. (DM 11 / Pargiter canto 91, web lines 195–196)
- **dm-event-055** — Devī predicts taking a terrible form in Himalaya and devouring Rākṣasas to protect sages; their praise establishes Bhīmādevī’s name. (DM 11 / Pargiter canto 91, web lines 197–197)
- **dm-event-056** — When Aruṇa troubles the three worlds, she will become innumerable bees and kill him, gaining the name Bhrāmarī. She promises repeated manifestations whenever Dānava oppression arises. Pargiter’s main text reads Aruṇākṣa, with Aruṇa noted as variant. (DM 11 / Pargiter canto 91, web lines 198–200)

### DM 12 / Mārkaṇḍeya 92

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117173.html)

- **dm-event-057** — Devī promises relief to attentive hymn-praisers and faithful listeners on eighth, ninth and fourteenth lunar days: no sin-born troubles, poverty or separation, and no fear of foes, robbers, rulers, weapons, fire or floods. Regular recitation secures presence in her shrine. (DM 12 / Pargiter canto 92, web lines 55–57)
- **dm-event-058** — Her exploits should be heard at bali, worship, fire rites and festivals; she accepts knowing or unknowing offerings. Autumn hearing grants freedom from trouble, wealth, grain, children and courage; recitation pacifies portents, bad dreams, planetary afflictions and child-seizure, reconciles broken fellowship and repels harmful beings. (DM 12 / Pargiter canto 92, web lines 58–61)
- **dm-event-059** — One hearing wins favor comparable to sustained offerings and gives purification and health; battle narratives remove fear and rebirth, hymns improve understanding. Remembering her exploits delivers from forest fire, isolated roads, robbers, enemies, wild beasts, angry rulers, death sentence, bonds, sea storms, battle and pain. (DM 12 / Pargiter canto 92, web lines 62–65)
- **dm-event-060** — Devī disappears before the watching gods. They regain offices and sacrificial shares; surviving Daityas go to Pātāla after the brothers’ deaths. (DM 12 / Pargiter canto 92, web lines 68–69)
- **dm-event-061** — Medhas explains eternal Devī’s repeated world-protecting manifestations: creator, deluder, giver of knowledge and prosperity, cosmic destruction, stability, good fortune and ill fortune; worship brings wealth, sons and righteous understanding. (DM 12 / Pargiter canto 92, web lines 70–72)

### DM 13 / Mārkaṇḍeya 93

[Directly read chapter](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-markandeya-purana/d/doc117175.html)

- **dm-event-062** — Medhas urges refuge in Devī, giver of enjoyment, heaven and release. Suratha bows and joins Samādhi on a riverbank; they make an earthen image, recite, offer flowers, incense, fire and water, restrict food and give blood-sprinkled offerings for three years. (DM 13 / Pargiter canto 93, web lines 53–57)
- **dm-event-063** — Devī appears and offers their desired gifts. Suratha requests regained rule with enemies defeated now and lasting kingship in another birth; disillusioned Samādhi requests knowledge removing attachment to I and mine. (DM 13 / Pargiter canto 93, web lines 58–63)
- **dm-event-064** — Devī promises Suratha his kingdom within days after killing enemies, then birth from Vivasvat as Manu Sāvarṇi; she grants Samādhi perfected knowledge. Praised by both, she disappears. Mārkaṇḍeya reiterates Suratha’s future birth as the eighth Manu. (DM 13 / Pargiter canto 93, web lines 64–71)
