## Extraction substance

The graph records Bhaṇḍa's requested strength-appropriation and enemy-weapon nullification separately from the granted sixty-thousand-year rule. Chapter12.53–57 distinguishes the expired royal term from Bhīmakarmā's continued reliance on combat powers; it does not silently turn all powers into a single expired boon.

The sequence includes campaign decisions, command disputes, night attack and defensive reorganization, Bālā's request and permission to fight, obstruction and its removal, thirst and army restoration, the two final brother duels, and the ordered chapter29 missile-countermissile chain. Entities manifested inside the chapter29 battle are context-scoped rather than merged automatically with similarly named epic characters.

Static chariot catalogues and army inventories have also been extracted in part. Repeated names in different tiers remain distinct contextual entries. Missing entries, ambiguous punctuation and defective spellings are exposed for collation rather than supplied from later standard deity lists.
