1. **gp-event-matsara-1** — Dakṣa requests origins and battles of eight incarnations; Mudgala begins Vakratunda. Indra’s annual accession festival assembles the named gods, sages, attendants and musicians recorded in mud-e-festival-roster. Rambhā’s performance and ball play enthrall Indra, who closes it prematurely. After forest play with Rambhā, seeing Durvāsas makes him leave her and bow; his seed falls on earth. He returns home, repeatedly condemns his distraction and resumes his own dharma. Matsara arises from his seed; Earth nurtures, names and initiates him at five. He studies Vedas with an unnamed teacher, and marries Durbuddhi’s daughter Madakarā. Outcome: Matsara reaches adulthood and marriage.. 2. **gp-event-matsara-2** — Matsara obtains the five-syllable formula from Sukra and propitiates Sambhu. He stays in a secluded forest, observes Pasupata initiation, fasts for a thousand divine years, repeats the formula and meditates on Sadasiva. His austerity disturbs gods and beings. Gods and sages seek Sankara’s protection; Sankara comes with Parvati and his attendants. They see Matsara reduced to skin and bones, covered by an anthill. Outcome: Siva reaches the ascetic.. 3. **gp-event-matsara-3** — Siva awakens Matsara and invites his wishes. Matsara regains strength on seeing him, bows, worships and offers a hymn. After Siva renews the offer, Matsara asks for universal fearlessness, devotion, immunity from death caused by the five-element and three-guna domain, health with lasting lordship, and sovereignty over the cosmic egg. Although the request astonishes the assembly, Siva grants all of it because of the fierce austerity. Outcome: The requested compound boon is granted.. The demons counter with their own account of Jalandhara, argue that gods are enemies of demons, and tell Matsara to leave Earth. He accepts this advice, which the narrator calls harmful, abandons Earth and stays with them. Outcome: Matsara abandons Earth’s counsel and resides with the demons; the campaign follows in1.25.. Virocana and chiefs themselves become Garuḍas by māyā and overwhelm Nāgas. Outcome: Māyā transformations overcome the first failed bird missile.. He recounts his own one-syllable worship and non-separation from Gaṇeśa, prescribes that worship, tells Śiva first to destroy inner Matsara then the demon, and leaves. Vakratunda appears as radiant Brahman, lion-mounted, elephant-faced, four-armed with noose/goad and boon/fearlessness gestures, Siddhi left and Buddhi right. Vakratunda promises the hymn will confer siddhis and desired objects and that he will remove Matsara. He explains that gods, humans, Nāgas and the world belong to the five-element/three-guṇa/eight-envelope domain from which Matsara has no fear, making destruction difficult, yet promises their welfare. He orders battle in his presence and says a form without the eight envelopes can kill him instantly. Gods and sages rejoice; the whole host goes to the city named Īrṣyā. Prahlāda and chiefs argue the defeated gods may have produced an illusion, dismiss the voice, and obtain permission to find and kill them. Frightened gods petition immediate protection; lion-mounted Vakratunda with noose/goad promises rescue and roars, terrifying all worlds and demons. Chiefs offer to fight while Matsara watches. 36. **mud-event-vak-boon-limit** — Vakratunda says he is neither god, human, Asura, Nāga, Gandharva nor mountain, nor a creature from varied birth-origins or the eight envelopes, but joy-giving Brahman. He came to restore gods and sages; Matsara’s boon-merit has loosened through accumulated sin. He offers survival if Matsara surrenders and abandons hatred of dharma and gods. Astonished, Matsara respectfully asks how attribute-free Brahman can have form and favor gods over demons. Outcome: Matsara moves from threats to a doctrinal question, without a physical final duel.. [Mudgala1.38.1–15](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_purana/mudgalapurANam1.html)

37. **mud-event-vak-impartiality-explanation** — Vakratunda explains creation as play with each being’s own dharma, his indwelling control through Puruṣa and Prakṛti, and conditional impartiality. When gods abandon duty to uproot demons or obstruct austerity, he inspires and grants demons boons against them. When demons abandon duty to uproot gods, he reveals their vulnerable means through the gods or takes form against demons the gods cannot conquer. He remains Brahman in both when they follow duty. Matsara oppressed gods through abandoning his own dharma, so he came against him. Outcome: The apparent favoritism is explained as restoring disrupted dharma.. [Mudgala1.38.16–30](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_purana/mudgalapurANam1.html)

38. **mud-event-matsara-vision-surrender** — Vakratunda shows his Brahman form in a point; by the grace and fruit of that vision Matsara receives divine sight. He sees the form beyond body/embodied, guṇas and māyā, with attributed body, attribute-free elephant head and their yoga unity. He bows, hymns the deity’s Brahman/yogic form, four arms, lion mount, Siddhi and Buddhi lordship, and seeks refuge and passage beyond worldly existence. He repeatedly falls at the feet and stands with folded hands. Outcome: Divine sight leads to praise and surrender.. [Mudgala1.38.31–50](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_purana/mudgalapurANam1.html)

39. **mud-event-matsara-new-duties** — Vakratunda declines to destroy a surrendered devotee, offers a boon, and promises Matsara’s hymn sovereignty, health, wealth, family and four aims. Matsara requests firm devotion, affection for Vakratunda’s devotees, and a place/livelihood with welfare. Vakratunda grants unwavering devotion and directs him to protect beings following their dharma and devotees. He must cause no obstruction wherever the deity’s image is worshipped or remembered at beginnings. Elsewhere he may act as an Asura, take fruits from karma/knowledge actors and rule in their hearts; toward devotion he must act as servant. Matsara accepts joyfully. Outcome: Matsara survives under restricted protective/obstructive duties.. [Mudgala1.38.51–62](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_purana/mudgalapurANam1.html)

40. **mud-event-gods-final-hymn** — Seeing Matsara at peace, gods and sages rejoice and hymn Vakratunda as Brahman encompassing and transcending forms, cosmic elements, deities, senses, creation/preservation/destruction, karma fruits, family and natural forms. They praise the demon’s transformation to peace and world’s joy, admit the deity exceeds Vedas, scriptures and yoga comprehension, and stand silent with folded hands. Vakratunda welcomes the praise.
